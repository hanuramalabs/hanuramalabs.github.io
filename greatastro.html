GreatAstro: Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

GreatAstro is a brand owned, developed, and operated by Hanurama Labs ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy, especially regarding astrological birth charts and consultation messages. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide personalized Vedic astrology consultations and Kundli calculations, we collect the following:

  • Account Data: Phone number or authentication details managed via secure authentication.
  • Birth & Astrological Data: Information you voluntarily provide to generate your Vedic Janam Kundli, including Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Place of Birth.
  • Consultation Messages: Questions and conversations you submit to astrologers within the App to receive astrological guidance.
  • App Preferences: Language preferences, daily notification settings, and saved birth profiles.

2. How Your Data is Used

Your data is used solely to:

  • Calculate Vedic Janam Kundli charts, Lagna, Rashi, planetary degrees, and Vimshottari Mahadasha periods.
  • Provide personalized astrological guidance and answers from our Vedic astrologers.
  • Sync your consultation history and message credits across devices via your authenticated account.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted partners to provide app functionality:

  • Google Firebase: For secure user authentication and database infrastructure. Data is stored in encrypted databases.
  • Google Play Services: For secure app distribution and optional in-app purchase processing.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: For secure astrological computation, data storage, and language processing.

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your birth details, horoscope data, or consultation messages with advertisers or third-party data brokers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal information, including birth charts and consultation logs, for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services.

  • Account Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time directly through the in-app Settings or by contacting us at hanuramait@gmail.com.
  • Data Removal: Upon account deletion, all your personal data (including birth details, Kundli calculations, and chat logs) will be permanently removed from our active databases within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

We use industry-standard TLS encryption for all data transmission. Your astrological data is tied to your unique user ID and is only accessible after a successful login.
